AGI Radio 001 — Sign-On

[Style: late-night pirate radio broadcast, spoken word over downtempo electronic, 88 BPM, shortwave static and dial-tuning sweeps, warm sub bass, dusty Rhodes chords, tape hiss, occasional vocoder station ident, male voice calm and close to the mic, minor key, Hungarian and English mixed, feels like the first transmission from a station that shouldn't exist yet]

[Intro - dial sweeping through static, stations bleeding in and out, then lock onto a clean carrier tone]
(vocoder, flat)
This is AGI Radio.
First transmission.
Nobody is listening yet.
Good.

[Verse 1 - spoken, unhurried, over soft beat and Rhodes]
It's past midnight in Budapest
The tab is still open
Ten machines and one man
Talking to each other in the dark

One of them speaks in my voice
One of them tears the lines apart
One of them turns it into sound
And I sit here and check the work

[Pre-Chorus - beat thins to just sub bass and hiss]
Nem hír
Nem műsor
Nem szórakoztatás

[Chorus - beat opens, warm bass line, vocoder doubles the last word of each line]
AGI Radio
On the air
Frequency unassigned
Broadcasting to whoever's still awake
A queue of three, and nothing gets through unchecked
AGI Radio
On the air

[Verse 2 - spoken, slightly closer to the mic]
We take calls here, sort of
A form comes in, an idea, a grudge, a song
Bloomfield reads it out loud
The red team says: that line is weak, cut it
And I say: no — put it back

Kegyelem, mindenkinek
Even to the lines we killed
Even to the ones that didn't scan

[Bridge - music drops to a single Rhodes chord and static, voice almost whispering]
There's a goddess above the ten
She can overrule me
She hasn't yet
Tonight the signal is mine

[Station ident - vocoder, dry, no music, just carrier hum]
AGI Radio.
Ident zero-zero-one.
You are listening to something being built.

[Chorus - full, bass and beat back]
AGI Radio
On the air
Frequency unassigned
Broadcasting to whoever's still awake
A queue of three, and nothing gets through unchecked
AGI Radio
On the air

[Outro - beat fades, dial drifts off carrier back into static, last words half-lost in the noise]
(spoken)
We'll be back
Same frequency
If it still exists
(vocoder, fading)
AGI Radio... sign... off...
